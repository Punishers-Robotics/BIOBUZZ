# BIOBUZZ 2026-2027: portfolio and judging boundaries

**Source:** Competition Manual TU10, Section 6, checked November 22, 2026.
See the [source and version register](../README.md).
This is a paraphrased planning reference, not an exhaustive copy of the manual.
These checks are not yet automated.

## Portfolio submission requirements

Source: Section 6.2, A201.

| Reference | Constraint |
|---|---|
| A201.A | One cover page including the team number; the listed optional identification/decorative items may also appear. |
| A201.B | At most 15 content pages, in addition to the cover. |
| A201.C | Only US Letter (8.5 by 11 inches) or A4 (210 by 297 mm) pages. |
| A201.D | A digital submission must be smaller than 15 MB. |
| A201.E | Progress, challenges, and accomplishments must be from January 1, 2026 onward. |

The cover does not contribute evidence toward award criteria. Content after the
allowed 15 pages is not reviewed. Use the cover for identification, not the only
copy of a required claim.

A201's accompanying guidance permits references to earlier seasons to explain
growth, for example in an organizational plan, but emphasizes the current season.
Label such context clearly; do not count older accomplishments as current results.

Minimize personal information. Student names must be limited to first names and
last initials, not full names. Student photos are permitted, subject to the team's
publication permissions.

AI/research assistance may be used with respect for intellectual property and
an appropriate footnote or endnote credit. Include an accurate AI-assistance credit
in a final edition that uses this workflow.

Source: A202. Follow the Event Director's submission method and deadline.
Without other instructions, provide one printed portfolio during the initial
interview. The event-specific deadline and delivery method are not recorded yet.

## Readability and evidence presentation

A201's guidance recommends avoiding text smaller than 10 points and low-contrast
text on images. Treat this as published readability guidance, not a separately
invented mandatory font-size rule. Establish and check the team's own readability
standard during layout design.

Judges do not follow portfolio links, websites, or videos and do not take extra
papers back for deliberations. Put the relevant explanation and evidence directly
on content pages. Repository links and code-revision references support internal
traceability but are not substitutes for a judge-readable explanation.

## What judges can consider

Source: Sections 6.1.1-6.1.4.

Judging uses the initial interview, follow-up interviews, and the portfolio where
applicable. It is based on published criteria and current-event/current-season
information, not reputation, external websites, past performance, tournament
rank, or robot scores unless specifically called for by a criterion.

Inspire, Think, and Control require portfolios. Connect, Reach, Sustain, Innovate,
and Design do not require them. A shared portfolio can support interview
communication, but do not treat every award as a portfolio-only evaluation.
Students still need to explain and, where relevant, demonstrate their work.

Section 6.1.4 favors sustained outreach over isolated activities and asks teams
to explain impact on those reached. Check FIRST's official Outreach Terms and
Definitions when making specific claims such as starting a team or reaching a
number of people; attendance and effort do not automatically prove impact.

## Event eligibility and award limits

Source: A213-A215.

Inspire consideration is generally restricted to the team's home region, with
exceptions for FIRST Championship and FIRST Premier Events. First-place Inspire
may be won only once across Qualifying/League Tournaments in the season; later
second/third-place consideration and the stated higher-level events have different
eligibility provisions. Check the full rules for the target event.

A team may win or be runner-up for only one team judged award at an event.
Targeting Inspire and Think is a preparation strategy, not an expectation of
receiving both at the same event.
