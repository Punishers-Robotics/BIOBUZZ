## Confirmed team priorities

The team's primary targets are **Inspire and Think**. Evaluate all three
Team Attributes awards (Connect, Reach, Sustain) and all three Machine,
Creativity, and Innovation awards (Innovate, Control, Design) to identify
supported strengths and gaps.

Inspire requires strong contention for at least one MCI award, one Team
Attributes award, and Think. This is not a requirement to satisfy every criterion
of every award. Do not choose a strongest supporting award before examining the
team's evidence. No award-fit assessment has been completed yet.

These are team priorities, not official instructions to every FTC team.
They do not establish eligibility, award likelihood, or judge approval.
Alliance, individual, and project-based awards have separate processes and are
outside this team-portfolio criteria map.

## Keep the reference current

Before preparing an event edition, check the official resource page, Team Updates,
relevant official clarifications, and event submission instructions. This reference
is a dated baseline, not a live mirror.

If requirements change, record the new source version, check date, and affected
criteria or rules. Reassess the page plan and evidence coverage. Keep earlier
season references separate rather than silently treating them as current.
