# Award-aligned portfolio editorial guidance

This workflow turns detailed records into concise, judge-readable narratives.
It is separate from [documentation intake](../intake-guide.md).
Start with the [confirmed award targets and sources](../requirements/README.md),
[criteria](../requirements/biobuzz-2026-2027/award-criteria.md), and
[submission rules](../requirements/biobuzz-2026-2027/portfolio-rules.md).
This guide is team editorial guidance, not an official scoring rubric.

## Select evidence before selecting claims

The primary targets are Inspire and Think. Evaluate all six supporting Team
Attributes/MCI awards, then choose the strongest evidence-backed narratives.
Do not select an award solely because an activity belongs to a matching folder.
Do not create evidence, embellish outcomes, or rewrite factual records to fit
an award.

For each proposed section or page, identify:

| Planning field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Award and criterion | Local criterion ID plus the official section/table reference; distinguish required, encouraged, and alternative topics. |
| Source | The relevant Markdown record and specific supporting evidence. |
| Support | Reported, evidence checked, or unresolved; assess how directly it supports the criterion. |
| Visible narrative/evidence | The explanation, result, diagram, or example judges can see on the page. |
| Page location | Proposed page/section, or `Not allocated`; cover content does not count. |
| Gap or follow-up | What is missing, uncertain, or needs student explanation/demonstration. |
| Review | Human review status and any limitations that remain. |

Maintain this mapping when planning real content. MECH-01 is the first populated
story entry; final page allocations and full-edition assessment remain open.
The mapping is an internal working aid, not a claim of award qualification or a
required page in the submitted portfolio.

Use the [starting page plan and story register](page-plan.md) to capture this
mapping. Its budget is provisional; source evidence and human decisions determine
the actual allocation. Do not treat reserved pages as required content.

## Make the award connection explicit

Use the published terminology where it accurately describes the work.
Make it easy to recognize the connection through headings, concise statements,
and supporting evidence, without repeating the entire criterion table.

Possible editorial headings, **not claims about work already completed**:

| Award | Useful language when supported |
|---|---|
| Think | Engineering process; alternatives compared; lessons learned and applied; mathematical design decisions |
| Connect | Skill-development goals and actions; meaningful STEM relationships; collaboration and knowledge sharing |
| Reach | Outreach objectives and strategy; FIRST community growth; successful recruitment |
| Sustain | Long-term sustainability; progress measured, reviewed, and tracked; future student leaders; risk mitigation |
| Innovate | Creative design; engineering development; stability and reliability; design risks reduced |
| Control | External feedback; challenge addressed; component function; reliability and engineering-process learning |
| Design | Whole-robot design; efficiency; maintainability; design choices aligned with strategy |
| Inspire | Inclusive student contributions; sharing knowledge; technical and team-attribute strengths supported by Think |

Follow an award-linked statement with the team's actual actions, reasons, and
results. For example, a "Lessons learned and applied" section should explain
what the team learned and what changed because of it, not merely list that phrase.
Use measured outcomes only when present in the records.

Do not claim "successful recruitment" from attendance alone, "external feedback"
from a timed sequence, or "sustainability" from a fundraising total alone.
Do not call a design unique or reliable without a supported explanation.
Clear award language is a signpost, not a substitute for evidence.

## Preserve the source of truth

Read the original records and their source/review status before summarizing.
Retain record links and criterion references in Typst source comments or an
internal planning map; make the substantive evidence visible in the PDF itself.
Judges will not follow those links.

If a fact needs correction, resolve it in the source record with an explicit
correction before changing a portfolio claim. Do not overwrite detailed records
with condensed publishing copy. Unresolved information should remain a gap,
not be promoted into a confident award claim.

One piece of evidence may genuinely support several criteria. Explain each
connection accurately while avoiding duplicate pages, duplicated totals, and
inflated reach or impact.

## Review the whole story

Check Think coverage and both Inspire supporting categories. THINK-1 requires
at least one of its four alternatives, not all four. INS-2 requires coverage
across the categories, not every award.

Keep team terminology ("mechanical" and "programming") for navigation while using
official engineering and control terminology in the narrative where appropriate.
Include students' choices and contributions, not just lists of accomplishments.

Review required criteria before encouraged ones, while preserving the team's
actual strengths. Ensure readable headings, captions, and diagrams, and place
award evidence on content pages rather than only on the cover.

Check current official rules and event instructions, then review the rendered PDF.
Template, font-size, margin, and page-allocation changes need deliberate approval;
do not silently shrink or clip content to make it fit.
Compilation, keyword matches, and an evidence map are not automatic award scoring
or publication approval.

Use the [portfolio review guide](review-guide.md) to assess a concrete edition.
Act on human-selected findings with observable closure conditions. Do not rewrite
the entire portfolio in response to every review or invent missing evidence.
Once closure conditions are met, stop editing unless the human changes the scope
or a specific regression or factual issue is found.
