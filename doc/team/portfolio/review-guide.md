# Portfolio review: consistent findings and human actions

This is an improvement guide, not official judging, online evaluation, an award
prediction, or an automatic rewrite loop. Reviewers report findings; humans choose
actions. Read the [requirements register](../requirements/README.md), linked
season criteria and rules, and [editorial guide](editorial-guide.md).
Use the [review report template](review-report-template.md).

## Fix the review scope before evaluating

Record the target season/event, PDF filename and revision or hash, source revision
(including uncommitted changes), criteria version, referenced record versions,
and any agreed readability/layout standards. Use the exact PDF being reviewed,
not a different build. State inaccessible inputs explicitly.

The default review examines Inspire and Think plus all six supporting TA/MCI
awards for evidence coverage. Other awards are diagnostic opportunities, not
automatic requirements to add pages. For an event-ready review, ask humans to
confirm which supporting award paths and remaining gaps they want to address.
Until then, the result is exploratory, not a readiness verdict.

Freeze that scope for a revision cycle. Do not introduce a new font preference,
page budget, or evidence threshold on a recheck unless the rules changed or a
human changed the brief. Published required criteria remain required, encouraged
criteria remain encouraged, and team preferences must be labeled as such.
Record any scope/version change and which earlier findings it affects.

## Review in a fixed order

1. Inspect the rendered PDF and measure available submission properties.
2. Check criterion coverage using physical PDF page numbers and source records.
3. Check factual consistency, evidence provenance, and unresolved claims.
4. Check whether award connections and supporting evidence are readable and findable.
5. Identify interview/demonstration follow-up and give humans actionable findings.

If the rendered PDF cannot be inspected, report a source-only review and its
limits; do not claim visual review or final readiness. If a referenced record
cannot be read, mark its evidence not checked rather than assuming support.
Do not change content, layouts, records, review status, or scope during review.

## Submission checks

Use [the portfolio rules](../requirements/biobuzz-2026-2027/portfolio-rules.md).
Report each applicable check as `Pass`, `Fail`, or `Not checked`, with the actual
measurement or observed location where available. Use `Not applicable` only with
a reason, such as digital file-size requirements for a printed-only submission.

Check cover/team number, content page count, page dimensions, digital file size
when applicable, current-season dates, privacy, AI credit, and event submission
instructions. Evidence on the cover does not count toward award criteria.
A generated PDF does not prove actual submission or on-time delivery; confirm
those separately with the human responsible for the event.

Treat readability recommendations and agreed team standards separately from
mandatory rules. Do not invent a font-size rule, claim a contrast measurement
that was not performed, or waive official constraints as a stylistic preference.

## Criterion coverage and evidence are separate

Include every local criterion ID from the season reference in the coverage table,
including the four THINK-1 branches. For each, state its level, page/section,
source record, coverage, evidence status, and a short reason. Judges' Choice has
no criterion table: note it as an optional opportunity without inventing scores.

| Coverage | Consistent meaning |
|---|---|
| Covered | Visible content addresses the relevant criterion elements with a specific explanation or example, not only a heading or keyword. This describes documented coverage, not official qualification. |
| Partial | Relevant content is present, but a criterion element or its explanation is missing or unclear. Name the exact gap. |
| Missing | No relevant content is visible in the allowed content pages after inspecting the PDF. |
| Not assessable | The input is unavailable or the conclusion depends on interviews, demonstrations, comparative judging, or other information outside this review. State what is needed. |

Track source support separately as `Reported`, `Evidence checked`, `Unresolved`,
or `Not checked`. State what checked evidence actually establishes; do not equate
human-approved wording with independent verification.

Apply the logical structure exactly:

- THINK-1 needs portfolio preparation plus at least one covered branch among
  THINK-1A through THINK-1D. Missing unused branches are not required-content
  failures. Actual submission remains an event confirmation.
- CON-1 needs both learning goals and steps; REA-1 needs objectives, strategy,
  and FIRST growth; CTL-1 needs components, challenges, and functions.
- SUS-1 permits one or more of its listed planning topics. DES-1 permits the
  stated alternatives; do not demand every design attribute.
- INS-2 requires all three categories but not all six supporting awards. Show
  documentary strengths and gaps by category. "Strong contender" is a comparative
  judgment this reviewer cannot certify.

Where a criterion involves real-world performance, inclusion, or communication,
report the visible documentary evidence and separately mark the actual outcome
not assessable if it cannot be established. A convincing paragraph does not prove
reliability, participation, recruitment, or what students can explain in person.

## Give each finding a concrete action

Use the criterion/rule ID plus a stable short issue name, for example
`CTL-1-component-functions`. Combine duplicate observations into one finding.
Keep the same ID on rechecks even when wording or page numbers change.

| Action type | Appropriate human action |
|---|---|
| Portfolio edit | Clarify or add evidence already present in records; fix navigation, captions, or a specific visible layout defect. |
| Documentation intake | Obtain missing details, evidence, or a factual correction; update the source record before making a stronger portfolio claim. |
| Team work | Plan a test, activity, measurement, or follow-up that has not yet happened. Do not represent planned work as accomplished. |
| Interview preparation | Prepare students to explain or demonstrate evidence that cannot be judged from the PDF alone. |
| Human decision | Resolve contradictory information, choose an award path, approve a layout change, or decide whether an optional improvement is worth space. |
| Submission action | Confirm event instructions, eligibility, delivery, or other facts not established by the PDF. |

Every finding needs its location, criterion/rule or agreed standard, observed gap,
action type, suggested owner role (not an invented assignment), next action, and
**observable closure condition**. For example, CTL-1 closes when the page identifies
each claimed control component's function and challenge using a linked source.
"Make it more compelling" is not a closure condition.

Prioritize without a numerical award score:

- **P0:** submission-rule failure, privacy issue, or demonstrably inaccurate or
  misleading claim. Fix before calling the edition ready.
- **P1:** missing/partial required documentary coverage for confirmed target paths,
  significant unreadability, or missing evidence needed for a chosen claim.
- **P2:** encouraged-criterion improvements, alternative award opportunities, or
  optional presentation refinements.

Unconfirmed supporting paths remain diagnostic; do not make every missing
supporting-award criterion a P1 blocker. Put interview, eligibility, and unavailable
input needs in their appropriate action queue, not an endless rewriting queue.

## Stop conditions and rechecks

Each invocation makes one review pass and returns a report. It does not launch
rewrites, repeat reviews, or change upstream records automatically.
Humans select a batch of findings to address. Recheck that batch against the
original closure conditions and scan changed pages for regressions.
Keep unchanged findings and closed items stable unless new evidence, changed
requirements, or an actual regression gives a specific reason to reopen them.

Track findings as `Open`, `Resolved`, `Deferred`, or `Awaiting decision/evidence`.
Deferrals need a human decision, reason, and scope. Deferring a required rule
does not make the portfolio compliant. An unavailable measurement stays not
checked; an unresolved fact stays unresolved.

If edits cannot close a finding because the record lacks evidence, stop editorial
iteration and route it to intake, team work, or a human decision. If the same
finding survives a focused recheck, explain the unmet condition and stop rather
than generating another stylistic rewrite. Do not keep adding optional suggestions
to move the finish line.

Use these overall outcomes:

| Outcome | Meaning and next action |
|---|---|
| Review incomplete | Required review inputs were inaccessible or the scope is not confirmed. Identify what humans must supply. |
| Action needed | A P0 or selected-path P1 item needs a specific change, documentation, or team action. |
| Awaiting human decision/evidence | Editing cannot resolve the remaining material issue; name the decision or evidence needed and stop. |
| Ready for human approval | Applicable portfolio checks and selected documentary closure conditions have no open blocking findings. Optional deferrals and external confirmations are explicitly listed. |

Choose the outcome in a fixed order: first check whether required inputs or scope
are missing. Otherwise, if a blocking action can be taken now, use `Action needed`.
If remaining material blockers depend only on a human decision or unavailable
evidence, use `Awaiting human decision/evidence`. Use `Ready for human approval`
only when there are no remaining portfolio blockers. Do not hide known findings
just because the overall review is incomplete.

"Ready for human approval" is the stopping point for editing, not a prediction of
award success or proof of full event eligibility/submission. Humans still approve
the edition and complete event/interview actions. If a required portfolio check
is not checked, do not use this outcome.

For explicitly scoped prototypes such as the one-story MECH-01 trial, record
that limited scope and assess only the selected story's acceptance conditions.
Keep every criterion in the report, marking unrelated ones `Not assessable`
with "outside the selected trial scope." List known event-edition gaps separately
without making the prototype responsible for a full Inspire submission.
This does not waive those requirements for an actual event edition.
