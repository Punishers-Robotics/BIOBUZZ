# Portfolio review report

This is an output template, not a completed review. Fill it for a requested review
using [the review guide](review-guide.md); do not edit the shared template.
Store reports in the session's artifact area unless the user requests a repository
location. Do not run revisions or mark human approval automatically.

## Review identity and scope

**Review date:** Not recorded  
**Season and event:** Not recorded  
**PDF filename and revision/hash:** Not recorded  
**Source revision and uncommitted changes:** Not recorded  
**Criteria/rules version:** Not recorded  
**Source records and versions inspected:** Not recorded  
**Readability/layout standards agreed with humans:** Not recorded  
**Confirmed award paths:** Not recorded  
**Review mode:** Full / focused recheck / source-only (choose one)  
**Previous report and selected findings, if rechecking:** Not recorded  
**Unavailable inputs and scope changes:** Not recorded

## Outcome and next human action

**Outcome:** Review incomplete / Action needed / Awaiting human decision/evidence /
Ready for human approval (choose one using the guide)

State the most important next action and why. Separate editing readiness from
interview preparation, actual submission, eligibility, and award prediction.
Do not fill in a numeric score.

## Submission and presentation checks

| Rule or agreed standard | Pass / Fail / Not checked / Not applicable | Measurement or visible location | Limitation or required action |
|---|---|---|---|

Include the applicable checks from the guide; label official rules, published
recommendations, and team preferences separately. Do not imply measurements
were performed when they were not.

## Criterion-by-criterion coverage

| Criterion ID and level | Physical PDF page / section | Source record | Covered / Partial / Missing / Not assessable | Evidence status | Reason or missing element |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include all season criterion IDs, including THINK-1's alternative branches.
Distinguish documentary evidence from real-world/comparative conclusions.
List Judges' Choice separately without fabricating criteria.

## Inspire and Think synthesis

Explain THINK-1's at-least-one-branch result. Identify documentary strengths and
gaps for MCI and Team Attributes, and any human-confirmed supporting paths.
Do not assert strong contention or require all supporting awards.
State inclusion/communication evidence and remaining interview needs.

## Prioritized actions

| Stable finding ID | P0 / P1 / P2 | Rule/criterion and location | Observed gap | Action type and suggested owner role | Next action | Observable closure condition | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use the guide's action types and finding states. Put missing records, needed team
work, and interview preparation in distinct actions rather than generic rewrites.

## Closure, deferrals, and human decisions

| Finding ID | Evidence of resolution, or remaining unmet condition | Human decision / deferral reason and scope | Next responsible role |
|---|---|---|---|

Preserve previous decisions and resolved findings on recheck. Explain any reopened
item with a regression, new evidence, or changed requirement. Deferral is not
compliance. End after the report; wait for humans to choose further actions.
