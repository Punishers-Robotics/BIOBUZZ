## Purpose

Use a deep understanding of the game rules to develop a winning strategy and
select robot capabilities that help the whole alliance succeed. The team wants
to solve difficult ranking-point challenges in ways that make it easier for a
wide range of alliance partners to contribute.

## Lessons reported from Exodus during DECODE

The team reports that Exodus won the World Championships and MTI during DECODE
and that BIOBUZZ competed as its alliance partner at MTI. These results and the
alliance participation remain reported, not independently verified here.

The team's main observation was that Exodus built and operated its robot to make
life easier for its alliance partner, regardless of that partner's capabilities.
The supplied examples were:

- Lifting during endgame so the partner could drive underneath to park, making
  the associated ranking-point challenge more achievable.
- Specializing in far-zone shooting, leaving the easier near-zone shooting to
  the partner and increasing collective artifact scoring.
- Proactively agreeing on a game plan with alliance partners before each match.

These are the team's observations and interpretations. Specific match examples
and evidence of their effect have not yet been supplied.

## Qualification and playoff priorities

The team distinguishes qualification ranking from playoff match scoring:

| Phase | Reported priorities |
|---|---|
| Qualification | Favor ranking points over a high raw score; avoid earning zero ranking points; win matches while pursuing other ranking-point opportunities; demonstrate value as an alliance partner. |
| Finals/playoffs | Focus on outscoring the opposing alliance rather than collecting ranking points. Ranking-point challenges may still be useful when they also generate score. |

The team wants a strong qualification position to help secure a complementary
playoff alliance. The notes emphasize that high scores alone do not ensure a
strong qualification ranking, and that pursuing a match win can distract from
other ranking-point opportunities.

The supplied notes describe **3 ranking points for a win** and **3 additional
individual ranking points from other game challenges**. These numerical values,
the exact challenge conditions, ranking/tiebreak rules, and alliance-selection
procedure have not been checked against the current BIOBUZZ rules in this intake.
Do not treat this summary as a replacement for the official manual or assume
prior-season rules apply unchanged.

"Avoid ZERO ranking points at all costs" is recorded as the team's emphasis on
qualification consistency, not permission to disregard rules or team principles.
The team's [GEAR principles](../team/README.md#team-principles-put-it-in-gear)
remain relevant to how it competes.

## Avoiding distractions and managing penalties

The team reports a DECODE lesson that chasing the pattern ranking point could
consume design effort without reliable payoff. In the team's interpretation,
successful teams focused on the other five available ranking points and treated
patterns as a bonus. The source also mentions shooting all purple balls as a
driving approach that could improve the chance of pattern points.

These are reported historical tactical assessments, not independently checked
claims about all teams, the winning teams, or game-rule probabilities.
The intended takeaway is to evaluate the reliability and opportunity cost of a
challenge rather than design around every possible scoring opportunity.

The notes emphasize that penalties can decide matches: avoid receiving them and,
where possible, draw them from the opposing alliance without losing focus on the
main goals. No specific tactic or rule interpretation was supplied. This records
the team's stated idea, not a ruling that any penalty-drawing behavior is legal.
Check the current rules and relevant clarifications before adopting a tactic;
do not infer authorization for prohibited conduct from this record.
