## Result and tradeoffs

The user reports that the team selected **only pollen intake and shooting for
the first MVP**. Pollen has the highest weighted total, 907, compared with 842
for Pollen + Nectar and 794 for Dual-Launcher.

The matrix rates Pollen lower on performance than both alternatives but higher
on simplicity and MVP readiness. Reliability and serviceability carry the largest
criterion weights, at 20 and 18. This documents the tradeoff represented in the
matrix; additional discussion or rationale beyond the scores has not been supplied.

This decision establishes the reported first-MVP scope, not completion of that
MVP, validation of its performance, or a permanent decision to exclude nectar
from later designs. A subsequent November 23, 2026 intake added the
