## Outreach categories

| Folder | Put these activities here | Useful evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Fundraising](fundraising/README.md) | Fundraisers, sponsorship outreach, donor engagement | Approved funds raised, in-kind support, results versus goals |
| [Mentoring](mentoring/README.md) | Sustained support for other teams or outside learners | Sessions, student contributions, feedback, learner progress |
| [Recruitment](recruitment/README.md) | Attracting students and mentors to our team | Audience, inquiries, follow-up, confirmed participation |
| [STEM awareness](stem-awareness/README.md) | Public demonstrations, workshops, community STEM education | Audience reach, learning feedback, follow-up interest |
| [Community service](community-service/README.md) | Service addressing a community need, whether or not STEM-related | Need addressed, partner feedback, verified outcomes |

Choose a record's primary category by its main objective, not just the event
format. A public demo focused on attracting team members belongs in recruitment;
a demo intended to teach the community belongs in STEM awareness. Sustained
mentoring belongs in mentoring, while a one-off public learning workshop usually
belongs in STEM awareness.

If one activity serves several objectives, keep one dated record in its primary
category, describe the secondary objectives there, and link it from other category
overviews. Do not duplicate records or add the same participants, hours, or funds
more than once to overall totals. Label activity attendance separately from
unique people reached; use `Not yet recorded` if unique reach is unknown.
