# Subject-specific intake guidance

Use these prompts alongside the [intake guide](intake-guide.md) and
[shared record format](templates/activity-record.md). They help interpret input;
they are not required fields or a checklist to ask every student.
Record only what is supplied or actually checked.

The [requirements area](requirements/README.md) defines the current award targets
separately. Use its criteria to recognize potential evidence and consequential
gaps, not to assume that a subject or activity automatically qualifies for an award.

## Mechanical

Destination: [mechanical records](records/mechanical/README.md).

Look for the mechanism or revision, problem, constraints, alternatives, changes,
test setup, measurements, failures, tradeoffs, and the resulting decision.
Preserve baseline versus revised performance and distinguish implemented designs
from proposed ones. Link available CAD, photos, diagrams, and test records.
Ask about units or test conditions when their absence makes a result misleading.
Update the current overview only if the current design or its known status changed.

## Programming

Destination: [programming records](records/programming/README.md).

Look for desired behavior, autonomous or driver-control context, relevant code
revision, configuration, sensors, libraries, tuning changes, test conditions,
repeated trials, failures, and recovery behavior. Distinguish team-written logic
from external library capabilities. Link actual code or logs when available,
without treating a proposed implementation as working behavior.
Ask which version was tested when results could refer to different implementations.

## Outreach

Destination: the appropriate [outreach category](records/outreach/README.md).

Look for the main objective, audience, date, student contributions, partners,
actions, participation, collection method, feedback, outcomes, and follow-up.
Distinguish activity attendance from unique people and effort from demonstrated
impact. Link one primary record across categories rather than duplicating totals.

| Category | Additional prompts when relevant |
|---|---|
| Fundraising | Purpose, cash versus in-kind support, pledged versus received, gross versus net, currency, and reporting period |
| Mentoring | Learners' needs, continuity of support, sessions actually delivered, topics, feedback, and observed progress |
| Recruitment | Student versus mentor audience, outreach channels, inquiries, follow-up, and confirmed participation |
| STEM awareness | Learning objective, public audience, demonstration or workshop content, feedback, and evidence of learning or interest |
| Community service | Community need, delivered service, units, approved partner feedback, and supported outcomes |

Use category READMEs for more detail. Do not infer impact from attendance,
volunteer hours, or positive remarks alone.

## Team development

Destination: [team records](records/team/README.md).

Look for training, onboarding, internal mentoring, team-building, collaboration,
student leadership, and reflection. Capture the purpose, participants' roles,
what students practiced or contributed, what they reported learning, any observed
changes, and follow-up. Qualitative reflections are useful; numeric metrics are
not required. Attribute reflections accurately and do not turn one person's
impression into a claim about the whole team.

External mentoring belongs in outreach. Recruitment belongs in outreach until
students or mentors join; subsequent onboarding and internal training belong here.

## Strategy

Destination: [strategy records](records/strategy/README.md).

Look for goals, priorities, match or season plans, constraints, alternatives,
resource decisions, milestones, risks, event lessons, owners, and follow-up.
Distinguish agreed decisions from suggestions and future targets from achieved
results. Ask whether a decision is adopted when the input is ambiguous.
Record fundraising activities in outreach and link them here when explaining
resource planning. Keep confidential budgets or personal details out of summaries.
