# Activity, test, reflection, or decision title

This is a shared output guide for AI-assisted intake or direct Markdown editing,
not a form students must complete. Use relevant sections, remove unused prompts,
and preserve consequential unknowns. See the intake guide and subject guidance
one directory above this template's folder. Do not fill out this shared file;
create or update the appropriate record instead.

**Activity date or date range:** Not yet recorded  
**Student authors/contributors:** Not yet recorded  
**Primary area/category:** Not yet recorded  
**Status:** Draft  
**Related current-details page:** [Area overview](README.md)

## Source and evidence status

Describe the supplied chat, notes, or transcript; include its filename or
identifier if known, intake date, and useful timestamps or sections.
Do not invent a source link or copy raw inputs by default.
Identify which claims are reported, which have specific evidence checked,
and which remain unresolved. Human review does not independently verify results.

## Purpose and context

Explain the problem, audience need, learning goal, or decision being addressed.
Include constraints and secondary objectives only where relevant.

## Actions and contributions

Describe what happened and who contributed, using approved public attribution.
Distinguish completed actions from plans. Include alternatives, tools, partners,
or outside help where relevant.

## Results and observations

Preserve measurements and methods when supplied: units, baselines, trial or
participant counts, dates, and collection methods. Qualitative observations and
attributed reflections are also valid; not every activity needs numeric results.
Distinguish observations, estimates, and reported outcomes. Preserve failures and
limitations. Mark missing consequential evidence as `Not yet recorded`.

## Supporting evidence

Link available photos, diagrams, logs, code revisions, feedback, or other records.
Explain what each item establishes and whether it was actually inspected.
Use valid paths relative to this record. Record assets are at `../assets/...`
from a top-level subject record and `../../assets/...` from an outreach category
record. Confirm permissions before adding assets or identifiable people.

## Learning, decisions, and next steps

Record supplied lessons, decisions, changes, or reflections without inventing
causes or outcomes. Distinguish adopted decisions from suggestions.
State follow-up and owners when known.

## Open questions, corrections, and review

Identify ambiguity, conflicting claims, and useful follow-up. For corrections,
record the changed claim, reason, source, and correction date; preserve history.

**Review:** Pending human review. Record reviewer and date only after explicit
confirmation. Keep unresolved or unverified claims visible even after review.
